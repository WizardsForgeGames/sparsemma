== 1. tests/bench_kernels.py --rounds 3  (primary; GPU time only)
Timing: each call captured n times in a CUDA graph (one replay >= 0.2 ms), 100 warmup + 300 timed replays,
events back-to-back, per-call = median / n; 3 rounds, median of rounds (spread printed by the script).
TOPS: dense-equivalent 2*M*K*N / time. quant = activation quantize kernel alone; sp GEMM = sparse GEMM +
dequant/bias epilogue on pre-quantized int8 inputs (fp16 out); sp layer = Int8Linear forward (quant + GEMM);
_int_mm = cuBLASLt dense INT8 GEMM (int32 out, no quantize/dequant); fp16 = nn.Linear FP16 (cuBLAS).
